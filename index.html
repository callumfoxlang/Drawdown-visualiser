BEHIND THE NUMBERS

What’s happening here?

We generate a fixed pool of illustrative monthly equity and bond returns, then sample paired months with replacement. For each simulated year, the average monthly portfolio return is multiplied by 12. Each portfolio is a fixed blend of equities and bonds.

At the end of each year, fees are deducted from the portfolio after returns, then a withdrawal based on the initial portfolio value is taken. Spending is either fixed or increased by inflation each subsequent year. A simulation fails if the portfolio reaches zero or cannot meet a withdrawal, including in the final year. The same sampled paths are used across portfolios and withdrawal rates.

Start age is used to display the end age; it does not change investment returns or introduce pension income. Equity and bond volatility are fixed at 16% and 6% annually, with a 0.2 correlation. A fixed random seed makes results reproducible; annual losses are capped at 100%. The model excludes taxes and changing allocations. It is not a forecast or financial advice.
